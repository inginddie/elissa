ELISSA - PRIVACY NOTICE AND LICENSE

Elissa runs on your PC: no account, no server of its own, no telemetry. Your settings and memory stay in
%APPDATA%\Elissa; your keys stay in the Windows Credential Manager.

To work, Elissa contacts only the services you choose, and only for the features you use:
- your AI provider (what you say or type, and what she reads for you); choose Ollama or LM Studio to keep it local;
- your speech-to-text service (your voice); choose "On your PC" to keep it local;
- Microsoft Edge's speech service (the text of her answers); choose a Windows voice to avoid it;
- DuckDuckGo and websites (only when you ask her to search the web);
- your mail server, Spotify and the integrations you add (only if you set them up);
- GitHub, to check for updates.
The wake phrase is detected on your PC; nothing is sent until you wake her.
Full privacy policy: https://github.com/inginddie/elissa/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
